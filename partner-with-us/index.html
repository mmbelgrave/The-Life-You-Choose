When referring can make sense

For people who do not yet know well what daily life they want to build, and whose question is still mostly about direction, wishes or different possibilities. For them, The Made Real Blueprint Phase 1 can be a suitable stepping stone, before they possibly invest in your product later.

Anyone who has already made a clear choice and needs specific information can come straight to you.

What your client can do with it

Phase 1 helps people describe the life they want, explore their options and write down a decision with next steps. That can lead to moving, testing further, waiting, or changing something specific where they live now.

The product costs €99 once, for the Introduction and Steps 1 to 3, made up of PDF workbooks and a web app. The workbooks are in English. There are practical examples, based on my own experience. Phase 1 does not give personal tax, legal or visa advice.

What you get out of it

  • A practical resource for a fitting early question, without having to develop an extra lesson yourself.
  • A clear way back: the client can bring a short summary and a focused question to you themselves. No data is shared automatically.
  • A fixed amount of €20, excluding VAT, for every sale made with your own discount code.

More returning clients or less intake work are possible benefits that we would test together. I do not promise extra revenue or a fixed number of leads.

Who is this for? Emigration and relocation specialists, providers around remote work, and communities that help people towards a different way of living. We also look at the overlap with your own orientation offer.

Starting small: first see where the value lies

First we look together at where exactly the added value lies, so that we can offer this in the right way on each other's web pages. Then we start with a link of your own that carries your discount code, to assess the cooperation. After that we can always look at how to extend it further.

For a community or platform there is also free material available, such as the free exercise An ordinary Tuesday and practical guides on the site.
