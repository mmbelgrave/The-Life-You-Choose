Who it is for

Is this for you?

This is for you if…

  • you feel a pull towards a different life, and want to do something with it
  • you want clear steps, not only inspiration
  • you are ready to look honestly at your life, including money
  • you want to choose for yourself, even if the answer is "stay"

What you work towards

Imagine the day you know.

No more going round in circles. Phase 1 helps you reach a well-founded decision, or see clearly what you still need to resolve. Each step gives you something you made yourself.

  • A written direction. Your picture of a new life in your own words, including how you want to spend your days, and the first changes that give you more control over your time: My Working Direction.
  • A researched shortlist. Places that fit that life, what you found out, and why the others don't.
  • A first money check. What the change could cost, what comes in, and how long your savings could cover a gap. In addition, a list of what you still need to check.
  • A considered decision. Go, not yet, or no, with your next steps. One you can explain to the people you love.

Who I am

I'm Mwata. I have built a new life abroad twice.

I had a good job and a comfortable home in the Netherlands, but something felt missing. A course helped me clarify the life I really wanted, and in 2021 I moved to Bali with only two bags. I built a life there from the ground up: a consultancy company, a newly built house and a local inspection business. After three years, however, my picture changed. That led me to build my second life in rural central Portugal.

I made this Blueprint because, although I had help with individual parts, nobody guided me from the first idea through all the decisions that followed. I did not need someone to decide what my life should be. I needed someone to tell me what to check first, based on real experience. That is what I wrote down in this Blueprint.

From my life into the workbook

What I faced: which region of Portugal, and which piece of land?

What I did: I travelled for two months in a campervan and viewed 25 plots. What surprised me was how connected I felt to one region, and not at all to another.

What I learned: you only find out on the ground. Go as a tester, not a tourist.

Where it is in the Blueprint: Step 2, Explore: test it in real life before you decide.

Read my full story
  1. 2020

    Selling the house

    I sold my house in the Netherlands to travel the world. Then COVID stopped those plans. A course helped me clarify what I really wanted.

  2. Bali

    Two bags, a new life

    During a two-month sabbatical in Bali, I finally had room to think. I wrote down a life near the sea, in a warm climate, where I could run my own business and help people. Back home, I left my job, sold what I still owned and moved to Bali with two bags. I started a consultancy, bought a house, and later started my own inspection business.

  3. Three years later

    A new picture

    Bali changed, and so did I. I missed being creative: building things and working in a garden. A YouTube channel about living more self-sufficiently set me thinking about another move.

  4. April 2024

    The third plot

    I travelled through Portugal in a campervan, exploring the regions and visiting plots I had shortlisted. I had not planned to buy on that trip. But when I saw the third plot, I knew it was the one.

  5. August 2024

    Signing

    I signed the purchase agreement. My money plan was already in place, so I was not afraid to commit. By the end of that year, the land was officially mine.

  6. Today

    Living it

    I live on my land in rural central Portugal, surrounded by nature, with enough projects to keep me creative for years. It is not finished, and it does not have to be. It is real.

What I stand for

The life you live today is shaped by the choices you make. Within your situation, you can make different ones. Not by dreaming harder, but by deciding, planning and building, one step at a time.

I build a life where I am present, dependable, and true to my values. I have the freedom to shape most of my days, spend time in nature, explore, and work on meaningful projects with the people I love.

Questions people ask

Is this a course?

Yes. An 8-step course with PDF workbooks. Phase 1 (Steps 1 to 3) is available now; Steps 4 to 8 are coming soon. The blueprint is what you finish with: your own written plan.

What does it mean to be a founding member?

The first 12 people who buy Phase 1 get it at the founding price of €99. In return, I ask for honest feedback as you work through the steps: what is clear, what is missing, and what helped. A testimonial is optional, and I only use it with your written permission.

Which option is right for me?

Not sure yet what you want, or where? Start with Phase 1. It works for Portugal and other places, but also for real changes at home. Already sure about Portugal? The whole Blueprint starts with Phase 1 as well, because a clear picture of the life you want helps you judge every choice that follows.

Do I have to move to Portugal?

No. Phase 1 works for any destination, and for staying where you are. Steps 4 to 8 focus on Portugal because that is where I live now and where I built a life.

What exactly do I receive, and how?

Right after payment, you receive the Introduction and the workbooks as PDF downloads. Each workbook explains the ideas, gives made-up examples, and has space for your own answers.

Can I start with Phase 1 and get the whole Blueprint later?

Yes. When the whole Blueprint opens, the price you paid for Phase 1 is deducted, so you never pay twice.

I already know what I want. Can I skip ahead?

I recommend still walking through all the steps: it helps you confirm the picture you have in mind. In my experience, most people still benefit, because it also helps you judge every choice afterwards.

How much time does it take?

About two to three hours a week. Short, regular blocks of time work better than one long weekend.

Do you give legal or tax advice?

No. Rules and prices change, so I always show the source and the date I checked. For legal, tax and money decisions, the workbooks help you find the right professional and the right questions to ask.

Is my English good enough?

The Blueprint is written in short, simple sentences for people who speak English as a second language. It explains every Portuguese or official word the first time. The workbooks are in English. Questions before you buy? You can send me a message in English or Dutch.
