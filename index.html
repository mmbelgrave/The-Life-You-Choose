Step 1 · Picture · Part 1

1.2 A normal day in your new life

Not a holiday. A normal Tuesday. Because most days are normal days.

We use an ordinary day because that is what most of your life will be. It gives you a useful question: would I enjoy the everyday life that comes with it?

Too vague to planDetailed enough to plan
"A house with a view""Coffee on the terrace, looking at olive trees, before anyone else is awake"
"Close to family""My sister can come for dinner without booking a flight"

Now you

Describe this day from morning to evening. Include an ordinary detail you would not put in a holiday brochure: housework, rain, or an hour when nothing much happens.

Made-up example (Maria, 46): I wake up at 7 without an alarm. From my window, I see olive trees. I drink coffee outside. In the morning, I work online for my clients for four hours. In the afternoon, I work in the garden or go to the village market. Two afternoons a week are for paperwork and cleaning, and I don't enjoy them. I go to bed tired, but happy.

I wake up…

Step 1 · Picture · Part 3

3.5 A first money check

Do I have money left each month, and if not, how long do my savings cover the gap?

That second number is your runway. Runway turns worry into a plan: "I am short 400 a month and have 40 months to work with" gives you a timescale for action.

Who I am

I'm Mwata. I have built a new life abroad twice.

I had a good job and a comfortable home in the Netherlands, but something felt missing. A course helped me clarify the life I really wanted, and in 2021 I moved to Bali with only two bags. I built a life there from the ground up: a consultancy company, a newly built house and a local inspection business. After three years, however, my picture changed. That led me to build my second life in rural central Portugal.

I made this Blueprint because, although I had help with individual parts, nobody guided me from the first idea through all the decisions that followed. I did not need someone to decide what my life should be. I needed someone to tell me what to check first, based on real experience. That is what I wrote down in this Blueprint.

Still a work in progress, but the idea from two years ago is now real.

Read my full story
  1. 2020

    Selling the house

    I sold my house in the Netherlands to travel the world. Then COVID stopped those plans. A course helped me clarify what I really wanted.

  2. Bali

    Two bags, a new life

    During a two-month sabbatical in Bali, I finally had room to think. I wrote down a life near the sea, in a warm climate, where I could run my own business and help people. Back home, I left my job, sold what I still owned and moved to Bali with two bags. I started a consultancy, bought a house, and later started my own inspection business.

  3. Three years later

    A new picture

    Bali changed, and so did I. I missed being creative: building things and working in a garden. A YouTube channel about living more self-sufficiently set me thinking about another move.

  4. April 2024

    The third plot

    I travelled through Portugal in a campervan, exploring the regions and visiting plots I had shortlisted. I had not planned to buy on that trip. But when I saw the third plot, I knew it was the one.

  5. August 2024

    Signing

    I signed the purchase agreement. My money plan was already in place, so I was not afraid to commit. By the end of that year, the land was officially mine.

  6. Today

    Living it

    I live on my land in rural central Portugal, surrounded by nature, with enough projects to keep me creative for years. It is not finished, and it does not have to be. It is real.

The first 12 people who buy Phase 1 get it at the founding price of €99. In return, I ask for honest feedback as you work through the steps: what is clear, what is missing, and what helped. A testimonial is optional, and I only use it with your written permission.

Which option is right for me?

Not sure yet what you want, or where? Start with Phase 1. It works for Portugal and other places, but also for real changes at home. Already sure about Portugal? The whole Blueprint starts with Phase 1 as well, because a clear picture of the life you want helps you judge every choice that follows.

Do I have to move to Portugal?

No. Phase 1 works for any destination, and for staying where you are. Steps 4 to 8 focus on Portugal because that is where I live now and where I built a life.

What if I decide to stay where I am?

That is a real result, not a failure. The option you test in Step 3 can also be a change where you live, such as working fewer days, a new job, your own business, more space or another region in your own country. If you decide to stay, Step 3 ends with your staying route: My Change Plan, with a date, a first step and a check-in. That completes your Blueprint.

Do I need the app, or can I use the PDF workbooks on paper?

You can complete the PDF workbooks on paper or on screen. To print or save your results after each step, and to get your personal Blueprint when you finish Phase 1, you enter your answers in the app.

Is the free Tuesday exercise only for people who choose Portugal?

No. Phase 1 and the free Tuesday exercise work for any destination, including staying where you are. Steps 4 to 8 focus on Portugal.
