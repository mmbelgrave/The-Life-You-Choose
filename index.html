The Made Real Blueprint · Portugal Edition

You have a picture of a different life. Let's make it real.

Maybe more sun. More time. More freedom. A garden, a project, a slower day. This Blueprint helps you turn that picture into a real life, one clear step at a time.

Two lives, built from the ground up: Bali, then Portugal. The method, written down.

Sound familiar?

The thought keeps coming back.

On a grey Monday morning. On holiday, when you catch yourself looking at house prices. Late at night, when the house is quiet. What if I just did it?

And then the questions arrive.

  • Where do I even start?
  • Can I really afford it?
  • What if I choose the wrong place?
  • What will my family say?
  • What if I regret it?

So the picture goes back in the drawer. Not because you do not want it, but because nobody showed you the first step. And next year, the same thought comes back.

I know that feeling. I had it for years. Then I learned to turn it into decisions, a plan and a real life. Twice. This Blueprint shows you how, one step at a time.

Who I am

I'm Mwata. I have built a life abroad twice.

I had a good job and a comfortable home, but I felt that something was missing. Work kept me so busy that I rarely stopped long enough to understand what it was. The feeling did not go away.

  1. 2020

    Selling the house

    I sold my house in the Netherlands to travel the world. Then COVID stopped those plans. A course helped me get clear about what I really wanted.

  2. Bali

    Two bags, a new life

    During a two-month sabbatical in Bali, I finally had room to think. I wrote down a life near the sea, in a warm climate, where I could run my own business and help people. Back home, I left my job, sold what I still owned and moved to Bali with two bags. I started a consultancy, bought a house, and later an inspection business of my own.

  3. Four years later

    A new picture

    Bali changed, and so did I. I missed being creative: building things and working in a garden. A YouTube channel about living more self-sufficiently set me thinking about another move.

  4. April 2024

    The third plot

    I travelled through Portugal in a campervan, exploring the regions and visiting plots I had shortlisted. I had not planned to buy on that trip. But when I saw the third plot, I knew it was the one.

  5. August 2024

    Signing

    I signed the purchase agreement. By the end of that year, the land was officially mine.

  6. Today

    Living it

    I live on my land in rural central Portugal, surrounded by nature, with enough projects to keep me creative for years. It is not finished, and it does not have to be. It is real.

The Made Real Blueprint · Portugal Edition

The guide I wish I had.

"I did not need someone to decide what my life should be. I needed someone who could tell me what to check first."

When I began building a life abroad, I could find help with individual parts of the process. A course helped me get clearer about what I wanted, and an agent helped me find land. But I could not find anyone to guide me from that first idea through all the decisions that followed.

I had to work out which country, what kind of company to set up, which bank, which plot of land, and which builder. I often wished I could talk it through with someone who had been there before.

I made this Blueprint to be that guide. Many courses stop when you are clear about what you want. This Blueprint goes further: through the practical work of building a life that actually exists, not just a plan on paper.

Good to know

Questions people ask

Do I have to move to Portugal?

No. Steps 1 to 3 work for any destination, and for staying where you are. Portugal is where the practical Steps 4 to 8 focus, because that is where I live and what I know well.

I already know what I want. Can I skip ahead?

Yes. A short check tells you where to start: at Picture, Explore, Decide or Prepare. Most people still gain from reading Step 1 briefly, because your picture helps you judge every choice after it.

How much time does it take?

About two to three hours a week. Short, regular sessions work better than one long weekend.

Do you give legal or tax advice?

No. Rules and prices change, so we always show the source and the date we checked. For legal, tax and money decisions, I help you find the right professional and the right questions to ask.

Is my English good enough?

The Blueprint is written in short, simple sentences for people who speak English as a second language. Every Portuguese or official word is explained the first time.
