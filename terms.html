The app has an AI partner. It is an artificial intelligence, not a person. It reads what you wrote and helps you think: it asks questions, challenges you and can suggest a first draft. Please keep these things in mind:

  • It can be wrong. Check anything that matters, especially rules, prices and dates.

If you choose to write them, the second box on the consent screen before your first chat is your explicit consent to that.

The AI partner is optional. Before you first use it, we ask for your explicit consent to send what you write to our AI provider, as explained in the privacy note. You can withdraw it at any time in Settings, and the rest of the app keeps working.
