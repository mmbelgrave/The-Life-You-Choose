Privacy note

How I handle your personal data

Last updated: 10 October 2026

In short.

I collect only what I need to answer you, deliver what you buy, run the app, and, if you ask for it, send you updates. I do not sell your data. The website does not use cookies or advertising trackers. The app uses only what is needed to sign you in and remember how it looks. I count website visits anonymously. The app's AI partner is run by Anthropic, a company in the United States, and it reads what you write to help you think. You can ask me at any time what I have about you, and ask me to change or delete it. In the app you can delete everything yourself.

5. The AI partner

  • The AI partner is an artificial intelligence. It is not a person.
  • It reads your answers, and keeps short notes about them to help you in later steps. You can read, correct, make it forget one topic, or delete all of it, under "What my AI partner knows".
  • It does not make decisions about you that have legal or similar effects. It only suggests. You decide, and nothing is written into your answers without your click.
  • I do not read your chats or notes unless you allow it in Settings.
  • You can switch off the AI partner in Settings. It then stops helping and stops making notes.

7. Your rights

You can ask me at any time to:

  • see the personal data I have about you;
  • correct it, or complete it;
  • delete it, unless the law requires me to keep it;
  • limit how I use it, or object to it;
  • receive it in a common file format, to take it elsewhere;
  • withdraw your consent, for example for updates, for the AI partner or for me reading your answers. This does not affect what I did before you withdrew it.

In the app you can delete everything yourself, and read and correct what the AI partner knows. For anything else, send your request to info@maderealblueprint.com. I will answer within one month.

Not happy with how I handle your data? Please tell me first. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Portugal, this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD, the national data protection commission), www.cnpd.pt. You can also complain in the EU country where you live.

8. Security

Your data travels over encrypted connections. The database is in the European Union, and each person can only open their own data. Pictures are kept in a private folder. The sign-in uses a one-time code, not a password. I keep my own accounts protected by strong passwords and two-step login, and I share your data only with the services listed above. If something goes wrong with your data, I tell you and, where the law requires, the data protection authority.

9. Changes to this note

If I change how I handle your data, I update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects you directly, I tell you by email.
