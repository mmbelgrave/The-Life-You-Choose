Privacy note

How I handle your personal data

Last updated: date of publication

In short.

I only collect what I need to answer you, to deliver what you buy, and, if you ask for it, to send you updates.

I do not sell your data. I do not use advertising trackers.

You can ask me at any time what I have about you, and ask me to change or delete it.

1. Who is responsible for your data

The Life You Choose is a brand of Belgrave Management, Unipessoal Lda, a company registered in Portugal. I, Mwata, run it. The company is responsible for your personal data (in legal terms, the "controller").

Company registration and tax number (NIPC): 518428338
Email: info@belgraveconsultancy.com

2. What I collect, and why

WhenWhat and why
You send a message through the contact formYour name, email address, "where you are now", your message, and whether you asked for updates. I use this only to answer you. The legal basis is your consent, which you give with the tick box, and the steps you ask me to take before a possible purchase.
You ask for occasional updatesYour name and email address, to tell you when something new is ready. The legal basis is your consent. Every update email has an unsubscribe link, and you can also simply ask me to stop.
You buy the BlueprintYour name, email address, billing country and order details, to deliver what you bought, give you access to the meetings, and keep the records the law requires. The legal basis is the contract with you, and my legal obligations for tax and accounting. The payment itself is handled by Lemon Squeezy (see section 3); I do not see your card details.
Your personal meetings, email and WhatsApp messagesWhat you choose to share with me about your plans, so that I can guide you. The legal basis is the contract with you. I keep short notes only if they help our next conversation. I only record a meeting if you agree to it beforehand. You can say no, and the meeting takes place in the same way.
You give a testimonial (optional)Your words, and if you agree, your name and photo. I only publish them with your written permission, and I say when a quote comes from a founding member who received a discount. You can withdraw your permission at any time, and I then remove it.
You visit this websiteThe website itself does not use cookies or tracking, and the fonts are stored on the website itself, so your browser does not contact Google. The service that hosts the website technically receives your IP address (see section 3).

Please do not send me sensitive information, such as health details, unless it is really needed for your question. If you do, I only use it to answer you.

4. How long I keep your data

  • Messages: up to 12 months after our last contact, unless you become a customer.
  • Update list: until you unsubscribe or ask me to remove you.
  • Purchases: order and invoice records for as long as tax law requires in Portugal this is generally 10 years; check with your accountant.
  • Meeting notes: up to 12 months after your last meeting, unless you ask me to delete them sooner.
  • Meeting recordings (only with your consent): up to 3 months after the meeting, and only shared with you.

5. Your rights

You can ask me at any time to:

  • see the personal data I have about you;
  • correct it, or complete it;
  • delete it, unless the law requires me to keep it;
  • limit how I use it, or object to it;
  • receive it in a common file format, to take it elsewhere;
  • withdraw your consent, for example for updates. This does not affect what I did before you withdrew it.

Send your request to info@belgraveconsultancy.com. I answer within one month.

Not happy with how I handle your data? Please tell me first. You also have the right to complain to a data protection authority. In Portugal, this is the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados (CNPD, the national data protection commission), www.cnpd.pt. You can also complain in the EU country where you live.

6. Security

I keep your data in accounts protected with strong passwords and two-step login, and I only share it with the services listed above.

7. Changes to this note

If I change how I handle your data, I update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects you directly, I tell you by email.

This is a draft based on the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR). It is not legal advice. Have it checked before the website is launched.
